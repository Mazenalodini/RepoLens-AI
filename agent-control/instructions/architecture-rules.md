# Architecture Rules

1. Respect the boundaries in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
2. Keep CLI/API thin.
3. Put business orchestration in Application.
4. Keep domain concepts framework-agnostic.
5. Isolate infrastructure details.
6. Keep Analyzers independent.
7. Keep Findings normalized.
8. Keep AI interpretation separate from deterministic measurement.
9. Keep report formatting separate from analysis.
10. Avoid unnecessary abstractions.
11. Avoid God Modules and God Classes.
12. Do not introduce distributed architecture into MVP.
13. Significant architecture changes require human approval and documentation.
